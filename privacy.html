Truvelio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Truvelio (Instagram: @truvelio) is an independent project that publishes sourced, fact-checked content. It is built with Claude and ChatGPT and is not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI. This app is used only to manage the Truvelio Instagram account.

What we process

  • Comments on Truvelio's own posts and direct messages sent to Truvelio, including the sender's Instagram username and Instagram-scoped ID.
  • Truvelio's own posts and publishing status.

Why

Only to read and reply to comments and messages sent to Truvelio, and to publish Truvelio's own content. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising, and we do not build profiles of users.

Retention

Message and comment records are kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate replies, and at most 90 days. Private messages are never made public.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send a direct message to @truvelio on Instagram with the word "DELETE", or email truvelio47@gmail.com. We delete all records linked to your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.
